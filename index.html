Inside Mac mini

Engineered to the core.

Scroll up to see the full exploded view. Here's what makes it tick.

Aluminum Unibody Enclosure

CNC-machined from a single block of 100% recycled aluminum. Provides structural rigidity and acts as a passive heat spreader across the entire chassis.

Antenna Plate & Shielding

Integrated antenna array for Wi-Fi 6E and Bluetooth 5.3 with EMI shielding to prevent wireless interference with internal components.

Cooling Fan Assembly

Custom-designed centrifugal fan with asymmetric blade geometry for whisper-quiet operation even under sustained workloads.

Heatsink & Thermal Module

Copper heat pipes connected to an oversized aluminum fin stack. Conducts heat away from the SoC to the fan assembly with maximum efficiency.

Main Logic Board with Apple Silicon SoC

The M4 or M4 Pro system-on-chip integrates CPU, GPU, Neural Engine, media engines, and unified memory onto a single die for unprecedented bandwidth.

Unified Memory & Storage

On-package LPDDR5X memory delivers up to 120GB/s bandwidth. Custom NAND flash controller provides up to 7.4GB/s sequential reads.

Internal Power Supply (PSU)

Fully integrated power supply eliminates the external brick. High-efficiency design draws as little as 5 watts at idle.

Rear I/O & Bottom Base Plate

Precision-machined port bracket houses Thunderbolt, HDMI, Ethernet, and audio. The base plate features thermal pads and vibration-damping rubber feet.
